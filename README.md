# The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue

A fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — three hundred years of rule, and the only ones who ever came back are the ducks in the moat.

## Entry

`main.py` writes a ~100-word monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. The tiredness lives in the rhythm, the pauses and the small deflections — never in a plain statement that the villain is weary — and the sting lands in the last line.

Standard library only, no network, no third-party packages.
